New preprint out! **Global Asymptotics of a Toda Soliton Condensate with Random Scattering Data**, joint with [Mateusz Piorkowski](https://sites.google.com/view/mateuszpiorkowski/home), is now available on arXiv. We study a dense Toda soliton condensate with random eigenvalues and norming constants, establish self-averaging, and describe its five macroscopic finite-gap regimes across the space-time plane. We also deduce the Nonlinear Dispersion Relation for the soliton condensate.

[Read the preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12748).
