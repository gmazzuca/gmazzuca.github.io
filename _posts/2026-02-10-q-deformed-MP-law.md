My colloborator Sung-Soo byurn, Yeng-Wang and I worked together to find the eigenvalues density of the $q$-Laguerre ensemble, which is an ensemble of particles randomly distributed on the segment $(0,1)$ with the following distribution

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

\[ 
    d\mathbf{P}(\mathbf{x})= \frac{1}{Z_{N}} \prod_{1\leq j<k\leq N}(x_{j}-x_{k})^{2}\prod_{j=1}^{N}w(x_j) \,dx_{j}. 
\]
